Who we are

Elite Rackz solves rack damage with engineering, manufacturing control, and field experience.

Elite Rackz designs, manufactures, and supports pallet rack repair kits, rack guarding products, rack inspections, damage assessments, and emergency rack repair paths for warehouse and distribution environments.

Our work is built around practical warehouse realities: forklift impact, bent uprights, damaged base plates, missing anchors, safety audits, customer reviews, and the need for repair documentation that facility teams can actually use.
