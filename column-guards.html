Forklift impact usually starts at the bottom of the upright.

Pallet rack column guards protect the lower rack upright, post, and impact-prone column areas from forklift damage, pallet jack contact, outrigger hits, and repeated traffic exposure.

Frequently asked questions

Pallet rack column guard questions.

Common questions about warehouse column protection, pallet rack post protectors, rack upright guards, and steel forklift impact guarding.

What is the difference between column guards and end-of-aisle guards?

Column guards protect individual pallet rack uprights, posts, and columns from forklift impact. End-of-aisle guards protect the exposed ends of rack rows where forklift turning, pallet jack traffic, and aisle impact are common.

Do your guards fit any rack brand?

Elite Rackz column guards are designed for broad compatibility across rack systems. The right guard depends on upright width, footplate dimensions, rack profile, setback, and the type of impact exposure in your facility.

What size pallet rack column guard do I need?

The correct size depends on upright width, frame depth, footplate dimensions, beam elevations, aisle traffic, and whether you need protection on the front face, side face, or lower impact zone.

Why choose steel column guards over plastic protectors?

Steel column guards are built for heavier forklift impact, repeated warehouse traffic, and long-term rack damage prevention. Plastic protectors may help with light contact, but they are not the same as heavy-duty steel warehouse column protection.
