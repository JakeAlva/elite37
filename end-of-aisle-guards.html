Why steel over plastic

End-of-aisle forklift impact guarding needs steel.

Plastic guards and light-duty bumpers may help with minor contact, but rack row ends often take direct forklift hits and turning impacts. Steel end-of-aisle rack guards are built for repeated traffic exposure and floor-anchored protection.

Frequently asked questions

End-of-aisle rack guard questions.

Common questions about end-of-aisle rack guards, rack upright guards, forklift rack protection, and pallet rack impact protection.

What is the difference between column guards and end-of-aisle guards?

Column guards protect individual pallet rack uprights and posts. End-of-aisle guards protect exposed rack row ends where forklift turning, pallet jack traffic, and aisle impact are more common.

Do end-of-aisle guards fit any rack brand?

Elite Rackz end-of-aisle guards can be customized for different rack layouts, frame sizes, footplates, and aisle conditions. The correct guard depends on rack profile, row length, traffic pattern, and impact exposure.

What size end-of-aisle rack guard do I need?

The correct size depends on rack row depth, footplate width, aisle clearance, forklift turning radius, dock door layout, and whether the guard needs left-side, right-side, or double-sided protection.

Why choose steel end-of-aisle guards over plastic protectors?

Steel end-of-aisle guards are built for heavier forklift impact and repeated warehouse traffic. Plastic guards may help with light contact, but they are not the same as floor-anchored steel pallet rack impact protection.
