Built for damaged front and rear rack posts.

The EZ Double Leg Repair Kit is designed for demanding pallet rack repair conditions where both frame legs need engineered reinforcement. Instead of replacing the entire frame, the kit creates a bolt-on repair path that restores the damaged section with a heavy-duty repair assembly.

Engineering and installation

Fully bolted repair without cutting beam end plates.

The EZ Double Leg kit uses proprietary beam reattachment hardware that allows for beam adjustment with roll-form or structural beams using original end connectors.

It does not require cutting off beam end plates, which can reduce beam capacity and compromise seismic structural integrity. The result is a cleaner repair path built around the rack system instead of a one-size-fits-all patch.

Repair vs. replacement

Restore damaged rack without unnecessary full frame replacement.

Full pallet rack replacement can create longer downtime, more unloading, more material handling, and more installation disruption. When the damage qualifies, an engineered double leg repair kit can create a cleaner path forward.

Elite Rackz can review your rack damage and help determine whether EZ Double Leg repair, another repair kit, full replacement, emergency support, or added guarding is the right recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

EZ Double Leg Repair Kit questions.

Common questions about double leg rack repair kits, lifetime impact warranty coverage, stamped calculations, and installation.

What does the lifetime impact warranty cover?

The lifetime impact warranty covers damage that compromises the structure of the EZ Double Leg kit itself. This warranty applies to all EZ Double Leg kits.

When should I use an EZ Double Leg Repair Kit?

The EZ Double Leg Repair Kit is designed for situations where both the front and rear rack frame posts require engineered repair or reinforcement.

Does the kit work with roll-form and structural rack?

Yes. EZ Double Leg kits can be configured for roll-form and structural rack applications depending on the rack profile, frame dimensions, and repair requirements.

Do EZ Double Leg kits include stamped calculations?

Stamped engineering calculations are available for qualifying EZ Double Leg pallet rack repair kit projects. This can help support EHS review, internal approvals, compliance records, and audit preparation.

Do I need to replace the whole frame instead?

That depends on the damage severity, rack profile, frame condition, load requirements, and documentation needs. Elite Rackz can review photos and measurements to help determine whether repair or replacement is the better path.
