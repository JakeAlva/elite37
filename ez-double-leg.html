Full product details

Engineered for the harshest rack repair environments.

The EZ Vertical, Cantilever, and Slant Double Leg Pallet Rack Repair Kits are built for demanding and harsh warehouse environments. The kit is designed to provide high impact resistance in a vertical leg repair system while maintaining the original rack design profile.

Fully bolted, the EZ Double Leg kit exceeds 50,000 lbs. of impact resistance up to 12″ high. It is designed to repair and reinforce a damaged front vertical frame post and a damaged rear post.

Proprietary beam reattachment hardware allows for easy beam adjustment for either roll form or structural beams using original end connectors. The kit does not require cutting off beam end plates, which can reduce beam capacity and compromise seismic structural integrity.

All EZ Double Leg kits are backed by a lifetime impact warranty. If impact damage compromises the structure of the kit, the kit is covered. This makes the EZ Double Leg repair kit a long-term repair solution for facilities that need more than a temporary patch.

Frequently asked questions

EZ Double Leg repair kit questions.

Common questions about double leg pallet rack repair, impact warranty coverage, stamped calculations, and repair fit.

What is the EZ Double Leg Repair Kit used for?

The EZ Double Leg Repair Kit is used when both the front and rear pallet rack frame posts need repair or reinforcement. It is designed for more demanding damage conditions than a single-leg repair.

Does every EZ Double Leg kit include a lifetime impact warranty?

Yes. Every EZ Double Leg kit includes a lifetime impact warranty. If impact damage compromises the structure of the kit, the kit is covered.

What does the impact warranty cover?

The impact warranty covers damage that compromises the structure of the EZ Double Leg kit. This warranty is designed for real warehouse environments where forklift impact and repeat traffic exposure are common concerns.

Can stamped calculations be provided?

Yes. Elite Rackz can provide stamped engineering calculations and repair documentation for qualifying EZ Double Leg repair kit projects. Documentation needs depend on the rack, repair design, site requirements, and project scope.

Does the EZ Double Leg kit work with roll-form and structural rack?

Yes, the EZ Double Leg kit can be designed for roll-form or structural pallet rack systems. The correct configuration depends on the upright profile, frame dimensions, beam elevations, damage location, and repair requirements.

Should I choose double leg, single leg, or roll-form repair?

The right repair kit depends on which part of the frame is damaged, the rack type, the severity of the impact, and the documentation requirements. Send photos of the damaged upright, base plate, anchors, surrounding bay, and beam elevations so Elite Rackz can recommend the correct repair path.
