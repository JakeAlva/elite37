Full product details

Engineered strength without cutting beam end plates.

The EZ Vertical, Cantilever, and Slant Double Leg Pallet Rack Repair Kits are made for demanding environments where both the front and rear vertical frame posts need durable engineered repair.

Fully bolted, this kit exceeds 50,000 lbs. of impact resistance up to 12 inches high. It is designed to repair and reinforce a damaged front vertical frame post and damaged rear post.

Proprietary beam reattachment hardware allows for easy beam adjustment for either roll form or structural beams using original end connectors.

The kit does not require cutting off beam end plates, which can reduce beam capacity and compromise seismic structural integrity.

Maintains original rack design profile

The repair is designed to reinforce the damaged posts while respecting the original rack design profile.

Works with roll-form or structural beams

Beam reattachment hardware supports roll form or structural beams using original end connectors.

Frequently asked questions

EZ Double Leg Repair Kit questions.

Common questions about double leg pallet rack repair, front and rear post repair, Made in USA manufacturing, impact warranty coverage, stamped calculations, and installation review.

What is the EZ Double Leg Repair Kit used for?

The EZ Double Leg Repair Kit is used when both the front and rear pallet rack upright posts are damaged or require reinforcement. It is designed to repair and reinforce both damaged vertical frame posts.

Is the EZ Double Leg Repair Kit made in the USA?

Yes. EZ Double Leg Repair Kits are made in the USA by Elite Rackz.

Does the EZ Double Leg Repair Kit have a lifetime impact warranty?

Yes. Every EZ Double Leg Repair Kit includes lifetime impact warranty coverage for damage that compromises the structure of the kit, subject to project-specific warranty terms.

What damage is covered by the impact warranty?

The impact warranty is focused on damage that compromises the structure of the installed kit. Warranty details are reviewed with the repair configuration, site conditions, and project documentation.

Do EZ Double Leg Repair Kits include stamped calculations?

Stamped engineering calculations are available for qualifying EZ Double Leg Repair Kit projects and repair documentation needs. This can support internal approvals, EHS records, and audit preparation.

How do I know if I need single leg or double leg repair?

Single leg repair may fit when the damage is concentrated at the front upright and the rear post remains suitable. Double leg repair may be needed when both frame legs require reinforcement. Send photos and rack details so Elite Rackz can review the right path.
