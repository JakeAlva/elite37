Full product details

Engineered strength without cutting beam end plates.

The EZ Vertical, Cantilever, and Slant Single Leg Pallet Rack Repair Kits are engineered structural repair solutions for demanding environments where a damaged front vertical frame post needs durable reinforcement.

Fully bolted, this kit exceeds 50,000 lbs. of impact resistance up to 12 inches high. It is designed to repair and reinforce a damaged front vertical frame post while working with an undamaged rear post.

Although the kit is built as a structural repair solution, it is not limited to structural rack frames. It can also be used on qualifying roll-form frames after review of upright profile, rack geometry, damage condition, beam elevations, and site requirements.

Proprietary beam reattachment hardware allows for easy beam adjustment for either roll form or structural beams using original end connectors.

The kit does not require cutting off beam end plates, which can reduce beam capacity and compromise seismic structural integrity.

Frequently asked questions

EZ Single Leg Repair Kit questions.

Common questions about single leg pallet rack repair, structural repair design, roll-form compatibility, front upright repair, Made in USA manufacturing, impact warranty coverage, stamped calculations, and installation review.

What is the EZ Single Leg Repair Kit used for?

The EZ Single Leg Repair Kit is used when the front pallet rack upright is damaged and the rear post remains suitable for the repair path. It is designed to repair and reinforce the damaged front vertical frame post.

Is the EZ Single Leg Repair Kit made in the USA?

Yes. The EZ Single Leg Repair Kit is made in the USA by Elite Rackz and is built for engineered pallet rack repair projects that need strong product support, documentation, and accountability.

Is this a structural repair, and can it be used on roll-form rack?

Yes. The EZ Single Leg Repair Kit is designed as an engineered structural repair solution. It can be used on qualifying roll-form or structural rack frames after review of upright profile, rack geometry, damage condition, beam elevations, and site requirements.

Does the EZ Single Leg Repair Kit have a lifetime impact warranty?

Yes. The EZ Single Leg Repair Kit includes lifetime impact warranty coverage for damage that compromises the structure of the kit, subject to project-specific warranty terms.

What damage is covered by the impact warranty?

The impact warranty is focused on damage that compromises the structure of the installed kit. Warranty details are reviewed with the repair configuration, site conditions, and project documentation.

Do EZ Single Leg Repair Kits include stamped calculations?

Stamped engineering calculations are available for qualifying EZ Single Leg Repair Kit projects and repair documentation needs. This can support internal approvals, EHS records, and audit preparation.

How do I know if I need single leg or double leg repair?

Single leg repair may fit when the damage is concentrated at the front upright and the rear post remains suitable. Double leg repair may be needed when both frame legs require reinforcement. Send photos and rack details so Elite Rackz can review the right path.

Will the rack hold the same load capacity after repair?

Elite Rackz repair kits are engineered to restore the damaged frame back to its original load capacity. The kit does not add load capacity to a pallet rack.
