Repair vs. replacement

Why engineered rack repair kits often win over full replacement.

Full pallet rack frame replacement can require more downtime, unloading, labor, and operational disruption. A properly engineered pallet rack repair kit can restore damaged uprights with a cleaner installation path and better documentation.

  • • Reduce unnecessary full frame replacement
  • • Repair damaged pallet rack uprights with a documented kit path
  • • Support compliance review with stamped calculations when required
  • • Pair repair kits with column guards or end-of-aisle guards to reduce repeat damage

Frequently asked questions

Pallet rack repair kit questions.

Common questions about engineered rack repair kits, stamped calculations, load capacity, and nationwide shipping.

What size pallet rack repair kit do I need?

The correct pallet rack repair kit depends on your rack type, upright profile, frame dimensions, damage location, beam elevations, and repair requirements. Elite Rackz can review photos, measurements, and site details to help identify the correct repair kit option.

Do your kits include stamped calculations?

Stamped engineering calculations are available for qualifying pallet rack repair kit projects and repair documentation needs. This is often important for EHS teams, internal approvals, audits, and compliance records.

Will my rack hold the same load capacity after repair?

Load capacity after repair depends on the rack configuration, repair design, installation, and engineering review. Elite Rackz can support load capacity review and repair documentation when your facility needs an audit-ready repair path.

Do you ship pallet rack repair kits nationwide?

Yes. Elite Rackz manufactures pallet rack repair kits in South Chicago Heights, Illinois and supports repair kit projects nationwide.
