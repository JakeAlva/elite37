How to get the most value from the checklist.

A checklist is most useful when your team collects consistent notes and photos. Use the same aisle, bay, and upright references each time you walk the rack.

1

Walk row by row

Use consistent aisle and bay references so damage can be found quickly later.

2

Take clear photos

Capture a wide aisle photo, full upright, base plate, anchors, and close-up damage.

3

Flag urgent damage

If damage looks severe, follow your internal safety process and request professional review.

4

Send it to Elite Rackz

Send photos and details so we can recommend inspection, repair, replacement, or guarding.
