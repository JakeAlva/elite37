Frequently asked questions

Pallet rack inspection questions.

Common questions about rack inspection reports, OSHA pallet rack inspection expectations, and stamped engineering documentation.

How often should pallet racks be inspected?

Inspection frequency depends on facility traffic, rack usage, forklift exposure, damage history, and internal safety requirements. Many facilities use routine internal walkthroughs plus periodic third-party rack inspections.

What is a rack inspection report?

A rack inspection report documents visible rack damage, affected locations, photos, severity, repair recommendations, and supporting information for safety, maintenance, and compliance teams.

What does OSHA require for rack inspections?

OSHA expects employers to maintain a workplace free from recognized hazards. Pallet rack inspections help identify damaged uprights, compromised components, missing safety pins, anchoring issues, and other rack safety concerns before they create risk.

Do you provide stamped engineering calculations?

Yes. Elite Rackz can provide stamped engineering calculations and documentation for qualifying pallet rack repair and compliance needs.
